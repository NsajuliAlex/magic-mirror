Magic Mirror

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

Magic Mirror does not collect, store, or sell your personal data. All information fetched during app use is displayed on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers.

1. Who We Are

Magic Mirror is an Android application developed by Alex Nsajuli, based in Belgium. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: ariaaltex73@gmail.com.

2. Information We Access

The app accesses the following data solely to power on-screen dashboard features:

  • Google Account (name, email, profile photo) — obtained via Google Sign-In. Your name is displayed in the greeting on the home screen. Your email identifies your account session. Your photo may be displayed on the dashboard. This data is stored locally on your device only and is never sent to our servers.
  • Google Calendar events — read via the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly scope. Upcoming events are fetched and displayed on the calendar tile. Calendar data is not cached beyond your current session, not stored on our servers, and never shared with third parties.
  • Outlook / ICS calendar feed — if you choose to connect an Outlook calendar, the ICS URL you provide is sent to our proxy server (magic-mirror-server-qxym.onrender.com) solely to fetch calendar data on your behalf and return it to your device. The URL and calendar data are not logged or stored on our servers.
  • Device location — used to fetch local weather conditions from a public weather API. Your precise coordinates are sent to the weather API and are not stored or logged by us.
  • Camera — optionally used for motion detection to activate the display when movement is detected. Camera access is local only; no images or video are recorded, stored, or transmitted.

3. How We Use Your Data

All data accessed by Magic Mirror is used exclusively to provide the features visible on your dashboard. Specifically:

  • Google account data → personalised greeting and session identification
  • Calendar data → upcoming events tile
  • Location → weather tile
  • Camera → motion-activated display wake

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose beyond the features described above.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party, except:

  • Weather API — your approximate location is sent to a weather data provider to retrieve current conditions. No personally identifiable information is included in this request.
  • ICS proxy server — Outlook calendar ICS URLs are forwarded through our proxy server to work around CORS restrictions. No data is retained.
  • Legal obligation — we may disclose data if required by law.

5. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Magic Mirror's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide and improve the dashboard features described in this policy. It is not used for serving advertisements, for credit-worthiness decisions, or transferred to data brokers.

6. Data Retention

Magic Mirror stores only the following data locally on your device:

  • Your Google access token (expires after 1 hour)
  • Your name, email, and profile photo (for the greeting display)
  • Your selected theme preference
  • Your ICS calendar URL, if you choose to connect one

You can remove all locally stored data at any time by signing out of the app or clearing app data in your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Magic Mirror is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For any questions or requests regarding your privacy, contact us at: ariaaltex73@gmail.com
